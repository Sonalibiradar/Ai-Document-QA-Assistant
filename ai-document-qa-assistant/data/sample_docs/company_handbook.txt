Employee Handbook — Acme Payments Pvt Ltd

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees may request flexible hours by discussing with their reporting manager.

Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per calendar year, in addition to
10 public holidays. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working
days in advance through the internal HR portal, except in emergencies.

Reimbursement Policy
Business-related travel and client-meeting expenses are reimbursable within
30 days of submission, provided original receipts are attached. Internet
and phone reimbursements for remote employees are capped at INR 1,500 per
month.

Code Review Guidelines
All pull requests require at least one approval before merging to the main
branch. Reviewers should check for test coverage, adherence to the team's
style guide, and potential security issues before approving.

Onboarding Process
New employees receive laptop and account provisioning within their first
two working days. A 30-60-90 day onboarding plan is shared by the manager
during week one, along with a buddy assigned from the same team.
